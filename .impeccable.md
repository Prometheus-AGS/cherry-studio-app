## Design Context

### Users
People who run agents from their phone: starting and following conversations, checking on work their
agents are doing, and reaching the rest of their devices while away from the desk. Sessions are short
and interrupted, often one-handed, in bright and dark surroundings alike. This is an operational
mobile app, not a marketing surface.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, utilitarian. The Boss orchestrates; the interface stays out of the way and lets
conversation, code, and the user's content lead. Follow DESIGN.md.

### Aesthetic Direction
The Boss Brand Guide v2.2, shared with The Boss desktop: a neutral-first cool ink ladder, depth from
the background, card, and popover surfaces rather than shadows, and one accent, ember
(`#E04E28` light, `#FF6A3D` dark). Four font roles: Space Grotesk for display, Inter for UI, Roboto
for body copy, JetBrains Mono for code. Radius 10px. Use CherryUI (`@cherrystudio/ui`) and the
semantic tokens in `packages/design-tokens`. Native platform conventions (iOS sheets and tab bars,
Android edge-to-edge) take precedence over ornament. Do not introduce a new palette, a second accent,
gradients, glass, or decorative motion.

### Design Principles
1. One primary object per screen; hierarchy through type role, size, and spacing before surfaces.
2. Show the effective state and a useful next action, not just configuration values.
3. Colour carries action or meaning only, always paired with a non-colour cue.
4. Every screen is complete in light and dark and at the largest supported text size.
5. Motion follows the DESIGN.md contract: responsive, interruptible, and complete when reduced.
6. Preserve entered values and keep work cancellable when an operation fails or the app is backgrounded.
